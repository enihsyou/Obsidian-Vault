## 后端接口

- 服务订单
充电订单最多显示 3 条，没什么用

```http
POST /moat/1100367/api/v1/otd/car/ext/general/aggr/orders?tz_offset=28800&appVersion=5.31.0&offset=0&limit=30
host: gateway-front-external.nio.com
authorization: Bearer
```

- 充电服务订单
一次可返回大于 10 个元素，但列表元素不包含充电量等信息，需要进详情

```http
POST /moat/1100367/api/v1/otd/car/ext/general/serviceOrder/getTabOrder?tz_offset=28800&appVersion=5.31.0&offset=0&limit=20&orderTypes=pe_shaman%2C%20pe_shaman_change%2C%20service_pe_discharge&inProgressStatus=false
```
