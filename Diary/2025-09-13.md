| :----------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------- |

| `linguist-generated`     | Excluded from stats, hidden in diffs                            |
| `linguist-language`=name | Highlighted and classified as name                              |
| `linguist-vendored`      | Excluded from stats                                             |
