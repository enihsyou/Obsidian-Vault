## PowerShell 管道会自动加换行符

#字符编码

数据经过管道 `|` 这么一传，就多了 `\r\n` 字符（在 Windows，平台相关）

```shellsession title="PowerShell"
$ (Write-Output "").Length
0

$ Write-Output "" | hexyl.exe --border none
 00000000  0d 0a

$ @"
> uname
> uname
> "@ | wsl
Linux
-bash: 行 2: $'uname\r': 未找到命令
```

- [stdin - PowerShell's pipe adds linefeed - Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/28504847/powershells-pipe-adds-linefeed)
- [Piping Text To An External Program Appends A Trailing Newline · Issue #5974 · PowerShell/PowerShell](https://github.com/PowerShell/PowerShell/issues/5974)
